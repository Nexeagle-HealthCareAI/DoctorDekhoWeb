“Hey Vita” recorder

Records short clips of real voices to train Vita's wake word. Nothing leaves this computer: the clips are saved as one .tar file that you give to whoever trains the model (tools/wakeword/README.md).

Press Start

About 4 minutes: 30 times “Hey Vita” (vary how you say it: normal, fast, slow, far from the phone, softly), then 15 other phrases. Each clip is 2.5 seconds; speak when the box turns red.
